How the value is calculated (model v3 — dollars per hour)

sizeAdj = 0.75 + 0.25 × min(tables/150, 1.6) · dayMult = min(1 + 0.6×(days−1), 2.2) · audience: sports 1.00 / mixed 0.80 / Pokémon 0.25 · risk: confirmed 1.00 / listed 0.90 / unverified 0.65

cardMargin = $290 × sizeAdj × dayMult × audience × risk. $290 is calibrated against your own logged results from two 2026 shows (figures kept off this page). Table cost is 2 tables, using the 2-for price where one is published. Mileage is drive × 0.85 mi/min × 2 × $0.30/mi; $140 lodging only if multi-day and over 2 hours out. Hours are open hours + 1.5 h setup per day + round-trip drive. The headline is net ÷ hours; the score is 10 × (1 − e^(−$/hr / 18)).

Defaults when unpublished: 80 tables, $50/table — those rows are flagged est. Shows over 150 minutes away with no published booth cost are not scored at all. Nothing currently inside the radius clears 7/10; Xenia is the ceiling at about $23/hr.
